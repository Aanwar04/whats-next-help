Privacy policy

How What's Next uses your data

What's Next helps you remember important dates and prepare for them. This policy describes the app's current local and account features.

Effective October 31, 2026 · Contact: anwarhussain.compsci@gmail.com

Dates without an account

You can use the app without signing in. Dates, notes, preparation steps, progress and local history are stored in a Room database on your device. The app does not send signed-out date collections to its Supabase service.

Signing in and cloud synchronization

Email/password and Google sign-in are provided through Supabase Authentication. Account information may include your email address, name, sign-in provider, membership and verification details, and profile-photo information. With Google sign-in, Google also processes the sign-in according to its own policy.

Signing in automatically attaches the current unclaimed local collection to your account. Account dates, notes, preparation steps, progress, trash and history synchronize with Supabase so they can be restored on another device. Offline changes stay on your device until synchronization succeeds. Different accounts have separate local caches.

Photos and offline profile details

If you choose a profile image, the app prepares a smaller JPEG and uploads it to private account storage. Images may also come from your Google profile. Account details and private image copies are cached on the phone for offline display. Local image-cache files do not store signed download URLs or access tokens.

Permissions and notifications

Internet access supports sign-in, account synchronization and profile images. Notification permission allows reminders. Alarm access supports reminder timing, and boot handling restores reminder scheduling. Android's photo picker lets you choose a profile image or wallpaper. Optional app lock uses your chosen PIN or device biometric facilities; the app does not receive the biometric template.

Appearance, wallpaper, app-lock and notification preferences stay on the device and are excluded from the cloud date graph.

Service providers and security

Supabase processes authentication, account databases and private photo storage. Google processes Google sign-in when selected. Connections to the app's Supabase service use HTTPS. Persistent sign-in tokens are encrypted with Android Keystore-backed storage and excluded from app backup. Account-specific database and photo caches are stored in the app's private storage.

The app currently has no advertising or analytics SDK enabled. This help website uses no advertising or analytics scripts; its hosting provider processes normal website requests. If you email support, your mail provider and the support mailbox process that message.

Retention and your controls

Account dates and profile information remain until you remove them or request account deletion. Trash normally has a 30-day local recovery period; edit-history and conflict alternatives may remain while the account is active. Device caches and archived local collections support offline use and recovery. An exported JSON backup remains under your control.

Deleting a date collection synchronizes its removal when cloud sync is active, but keeps the account and may keep saved conflict alternatives. To remove the account and its associated cloud data, follow the account-deletion instructions.

In-app account deletion clears the deleting account's local caches and assigned archives on that phone after server confirmation. For other devices or email-based deletion requests, remove local copies through Android's app storage controls. Independent local collections and user-exported files are separate. A non-identifying completion receipt may be kept for safe deletion retries; completed receipts contain no account UUID, email, dates or profile.

Questions or requests

Contact anwarhussain.compsci@gmail.com for privacy questions, corrections or deletion requests. We may verify that you control an account before changing or deleting its data.
