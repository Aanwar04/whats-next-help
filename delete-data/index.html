Data control

Delete data. Keep your account.

What's Next lets you remove your current date collection without deleting your sign-in account.

Delete your date collection in the app

  1. Open What's Next and select the collection you want to clear. If you want to remove your account's current cloud dates, sign in to that account first.
  2. Open Settings and scroll to Danger Zone.
  3. Choose Delete date collection, read the confirmation, and select Delete Everything.
  4. If Cloud dates is active, reconnect to the internet and let synchronization finish. Other devices need to connect and synchronize to receive the removal.

Export a JSON backup first if you want to keep a copy. This action keeps your account active.

Request data deletion by email

  1. Email anwarhussain.compsci@gmail.com from the address linked to your What's Next account.
  2. Use the subject Delete my What's Next data - keep my account. State which dates or other account data you want removed, and explicitly say that you want to keep your account.
  3. Support will verify account ownership and confirm the scope and outcome. You do not need to sign in to this website to send a request.
Open data-deletion request email

This opens a draft. Send the email to submit your request.

Do not send passwords, app PINs, verification codes or sign-in tokens.

What collection deletion removes

  • The active collection's dates, notes and preparation steps.
  • Checklist progress, trash, occurrence records and local edit history in that collection.
  • The collection's settings are reset, and its scheduled reminders are canceled.

When Cloud dates is active, the cleared current collection synchronizes to your account. Signed-out collection deletion affects local data on that phone.

What is kept and for how long

  • Your sign-in account, email, profile details and profile photo remain while your account is active, unless separately changed or deleted.
  • Other collections, assigned local archives and saved conflict alternatives are not erased by the collection-deletion action.
  • Historical cloud synchronization request records can retain previously synced content. These records and saved conflict alternatives do not currently have an automatic expiry and may remain while your account exists. Specify retained copies in your email request if you want help removing them without closing your account.
  • Offline devices can retain local copies until they reconnect or you remove those copies through Android's app storage controls. Backups, exports and screenshots that you created remain under your control.

The collection-deletion action has no additional recovery or trash period. Cloud removal of the current collection waits for successful synchronization. To remove the account and associated cloud records instead, follow the account-deletion instructions.
