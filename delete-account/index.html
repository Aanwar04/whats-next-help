Account control

Delete your What's Next account

You can request account deletion here without signing in to this website or reinstalling the app.

Send a deletion request

  1. Email support from the address linked to your What's Next account.
  2. Use the subject Delete my What's Next account and include your account email.
  3. We will verify account ownership before processing the request. Support will reply if more information is needed and confirm the outcome.
Open deletion-request email

This opens a draft in your email app. Send the email to submit the request. You can also write directly to anwarhussain.compsci@gmail.com.

Do not send your password, app PIN, verification codes or sign-in tokens. We do not need them to verify your request.

What deletion removes

  • Your What's Next sign-in account and profile information.
  • Cloud dates, preparation checklists and progress, trash, and saved cloud history associated with the account.
  • Profile photos stored for the account.

Deleting your What's Next account leaves your Google account active. Other users' accounts and their data are unaffected.

Copies on your devices

In-app account deletion also removes that account's cached dates, saved versions, profile-photo copies and assigned local archives from the phone performing the deletion. Independent, unclaimed local dates are kept.

For an offline device or a deletion requested by email, remove its local copy through Android's app storage controls when you have finished exporting anything you want to keep. JSON backups, screenshots and other copies you exported are under your control.

Before you request deletion

Export a backup from the app if you want to keep your dates. Account deletion is permanent. A small, non-identifying completion receipt may remain for safe retries; completed receipts contain no account email, profile, dates or account UUID.

Using the app

In versions with the account-deletion control, open your profile menu, choose Account, then Delete account. If the control is unavailable or cannot finish, use the email request above.
